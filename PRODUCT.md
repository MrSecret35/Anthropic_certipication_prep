# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User specified React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Lucide React, React Router; static GitHub Pages deployment.
## Users
English- and Italian-speaking learners preparing for Claude certifications.
## Product Purpose
A free independent certification preparation app with structured study, original practice, timed exams, and deterministic module-specific review recommendations.
## Operating Context
No login, backend, AI API, or required paid service. Progress stays in localStorage and is portable as JSON.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Four independent certification paths. Source official facts; mark unavailable blueprints unverified. Prefer substantive starter content over inflated banks. Full exams in English. Exact question-to-module mapping closes Study → Practice → Exam → Review → Retest.
## Brand Commitments
User selected a study atlas: light paper, ultramarine ink, and cartographic rules, with the editorial spaciousness of Anthropic as a reference. Fieldnotes has its own mark and identity. Keep the persistent navigation and accessible light and dark themes.
## Evidence on Hand
Detailed user brief. Official Partner Academy pages block direct retrieval; public FAQ is accessible. Never invent certification blueprints or claim authentic exam questions.
## Product Principles
Accuracy before quantity. Learning before metrics. Honest sample sizes. Local ownership of data.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Responsive web, keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic controls, reduced motion, English and Italian content.
