# Fieldnotes design system
The user approved a quiet editorial study workspace in warm neutrals with terracotta accents and a persistent sidebar. This commitment takes priority over automated direction assignment. Mode: Operate for navigation/exams, Read for lessons.

Self-hosted Lora for page titles and reading emphasis; DM Sans for interface and body. Ink on paper, fine warm borders, forest green for completed work. Flat surfaces, almost no shadow, 6–12px corner radii, generous whitespace. Sidebar 236px; content maximum 1180px. Cards represent certifications; lesson lists use ruled rows. A small notebook-style emblem provides identity without mimicking official marks. Desktop and mobile share semantics and adapt navigation into a compact header/menu. Dark mode uses charcoal paper and warm off-white text.

Signature: a simple numbered learning loop and a terracotta recommended study panel. Empty progress is honest. Domain weighting is always sourced, performance always shows sample size. Reading columns stay below 76 characters. No stock illustrations or decorative charts. Focus is visible, status is textual, reduced motion is respected.
